Privacy notice

DRAFT FOR UAT — this notice is pending review and approval by the organizer's Legal team (decision D-12/D-13). It is not yet the final legal text.

What we collect

When you create an account, register, request a booth, send a contact message or attend the event we process: your name, verified email or phone, company, job title and country; your registrations, booth requests, invoices and verified payments; your consent version and time; and check-in records at event gates.

Why

To verify your account, manage registrations and booth requests, issue invoices for manual bank transfer, confirm payments, issue and validate personal QR passes, respond to enquiries and report anonymised attendance. Your QR code contains only a random token, never your name or contact details.

Who can see it

Only authorised organizer staff, according to their role (for example, Finance sees invoices and payments; gate staff see only what is needed to admit you). Exhibitor names are published on the floorplan only after approval.

Retention and your rights

Retention periods and the procedure for access, correction and deletion requests will be published here once approved. Until then, contact the organizer through the contact page.
