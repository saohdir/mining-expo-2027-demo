Registration & payment terms

DRAFT FOR UAT — pending approval of prices, deposit, cancellation and refund policy (decisions D-01, D-02, D-03, D-12). No live sales are accepted until approved.

Payments

There is no online card or payment-gateway checkout. Paid conference seats and booths are invoiced by the organizer and paid by manual bank transfer to the account shown on your official invoice in your account. Uploading a transfer receipt does not confirm payment; Finance confirms receipt from the bank statement.

Booths

Choosing booths on the floorplan and entering a requested area is a request only. The organizer confirms booth units, area and price in a quotation you accept; booth allocation is confirmed separately by Operations.

Cancellations and refunds

The cancellation and refund policy will be published here once approved by the organizer.
