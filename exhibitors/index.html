WHY EXHIBIT?

Showcase. Connect. Grow.

Position your brand at the center of Indonesia-China's mining and energy business, meet qualified buyers, and generate real opportunities for partnerships, investment, and new projects.

EXHIBITION SCOPE

A world of opportunities.

A comprehensive showcase across the mining and energy value chain, connecting technologies, equipment, and solutions for a more efficient, safer, and sustainable industry.

  • Mining Equipment

    Heavy equipment and machines for exploration, mining, and processing.

  • Mineral Processing

    Crushing, grinding, separation, and refining technologies.

  • Metallurgy & Smelting

    Smelting, refining, and value-added metal production solutions.

  • Energy & Power

    Power generation, energy supply, and integrated energy solutions for mining.

  • Smart Mining & Digitalization

    Automation, IoT, AI, and digital solutions for safer, smarter, and more efficient mining.

  • Green Mining & ESG

    Environmental protection, resource efficiency, reclamation, and sustainable mining practices.

  • Supporting Services

    EPC, engineering, consulting, logistics, finance, and other professional services.

  • Mineral Resources & Products

    View mineral resources and related products, including nickel, copper, bauxite, and more.
